Movies & TV module

Phone-first layout (bottom nav). Structure is shared across both ambiances — Screening Room (dark) and Reading Room (light) — shown side-by-side on Home & Discover; all other screens render in Screening Room with the Reading Room / desktop adaptation noted in each caption.

Screening Room · champagne gold
Reading Room · rust / terracotta
Bodoni Modadisplay · Geistbody
Status colors Watchlist — the committed 'yes' (module accent) Save / Maybe — tentative (dusty rose) Watched — completed (cool steel) Reading Room set: rust · copper-rose · slate blue. Books' wine/forest reserved — unused here.
Nav decision — persistent 5-tab bottom nav (Home · Discover · Search · Your space · Calendar) on phone; on desktop/Windows the same 5 destinations become a left nav rail, and the Movies|TV segmented toggle moves into the top app bar. Discover keeps a prominent tab because it's the signature interaction, not something to bury in a menu.

Breakpoint notes — M3 Compact (<600) = the phone frames above. Medium (600–839) and up switch to a left nav rail; Large (1200–1599) shown here. Expanded (840–1199) inherits the Large two-pane pattern at slightly tighter gutters; Extra-large (1600+) inherits Large with wider max-width and more breathing room — no structural change. Search, Browse, and Calendar aren't re-drawn: their single-column layouts simply center in a max-width column (the filter sheet becomes a right-docked panel on Large), so no layout logic changes.
